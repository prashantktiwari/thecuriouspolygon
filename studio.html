A few practical details.

Covers and portrait slots accept images. Project film and process slots accept images, MP4/WebM, YouTube and Instagram. Remote videos load only when a visitor chooses to play. Instagram opens the original post; it does not need a fragile embedded player.

Image: WebP/JPG/PNG, ideally 1600px wide and under 400 KB. Video: H.264 MP4 or WebM, ideally under 20 MB. Add captions when speech matters. Export includes all media settings and any local files selected in this session. Unexported edits are lost when this page reloads.
