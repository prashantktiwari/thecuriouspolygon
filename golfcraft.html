01 / THE CHALLENGE

Creating many courses meant balancing visual variation with the constraints of the in-house physics engine and the Decentraland SDK.

MY CONTRIBUTION
  • Designed 290+ golf course levels.

  • Worked with the art director on custom assets and visual consistency.
  • Prepared levels around the game’s technical and gameplay constraints.
THE OUTCOME

A catalogue of 290+ designed levels, supported by modular assets and a repeatable production workflow. The emphasis here is my level-design and tooling contribution.
