01 / THE CHALLENGE

Make it work.
Make it repeatable.

A drone show company needs a credible operating system alongside the creative output: documented responsibilities, consistent procedures, inspection readiness and a demonstration of the operation.

MY CONTRIBUTION
  • Developed and coordinated the company’s operating manuals and certification documentation.
  • Prepared operational processes and materials for NCAA review and inspection.
  • Coordinated the demonstration process, connecting personnel, equipment, site preparation and show execution.
  • Helped secure the company’s NCAA RPAS Operator Certificate, received in October 2026.
THE OUTCOME

The operator’s NCAA RPAS Operator Certificate secured in October 2026. My contribution connected documentation, inspection readiness and the live demonstration process. The certificate belongs to the company.
