01 / THE CHALLENGE

Make it work.
Make it repeatable.

Delivering complex shows under tight deadlines required more than individual animations. Timing, transitions, assets and reviews needed to work together as a reliable production system.

MY CONTRIBUTION
  • Built and led a Blender / Geometry Nodes based drone light show pipeline.
  • Standardised timing, transitions and reusable assets to make revisions repeatable.
  • Led 10+ designers with review checkpoints, workload allocation and training.
  • Coordinated creative and engineering requirements, including last-minute client changes.
THE OUTCOME

Creative leadership across 150+ delivered shows, with projects involving 750+ drones, and a design team of 10+. Repeatable workflows supported the team through tight deadlines and client revisions.
